About The Host

Travel is a
Way of Life

Featured on VoyageATL, Virgilio Modica JR brings Deaf travelers together for intimate, land-based culture and travel adventures around the world (18+ countries traveled).

Small Intimate Groups

Trips are intentionally kept to small, friendly groups (6–8 people max) to ensure genuine connection, relaxed travel, and fun memories.

Direct Communication

Direct visual sign language communication and friendly local interactions everywhere we travel, ensuring an inclusive experience for all.
